## Description

Copy everything between the lines.

---

About 1 in 12 men is red-green color blind, and many more people live with blurry, low-contrast or narrowed vision. Vision Accessibility Suite shows you how your design looks to them, right inside Figma, so you can catch problems before your users do.

Works in Figma Design.


✨ What it does

Quick simulation: select a layer and click a simulation. A filtered copy appears right next to your design, in a neat row. Or click "Add all simulations" to get all 14 at once.

Vision Lab: a live preview of your selection. Stack simulations on top of each other to recreate real-life situations, tune each one with sliders, zoom in and pan around, then add the result to your canvas.

Presets: start from realistic combinations like Advanced Cataracts, Aging Vision (65+ and 80+), Outdoor Viewing or Night Vision, or save your own. Your presets are saved to your account, so they're there in every file.


👁️ 14 simulations

Color blindness
- Protanopia and protanomaly (red)
- Deuteranopia and deuteranomaly (green, the most common)
- Tritanopia and tritanomaly (blue)
- Achromatopsia (no color vision)
"-opia" means a color can't be seen at all, "-omaly" that it's seen weakly. Partial types have a severity slider.

Visual field
- Central loss (macular degeneration)
- Peripheral loss (glaucoma, tunnel vision)

Other
- Blurred vision, double vision (ghosting), yellowing (aging lens), loss of contrast, bright light


📖 How to use

Quick simulation:
1. Select a frame, component or any layer
2. Click a simulation (hover a card to see what it simulates), or "Add all simulations"

Vision Lab:
1. Open the Vision Lab tab and select a layer
2. Pick a preset in "Start from", or click "+ Add a simulation"
3. Adjust the sliders, reorder the simulations, and zoom or drag the preview
4. Click "Add current view to Figma", or "Save as preset" to keep the combination


🔧 Good to know
- Color blindness uses the Machado et al. (2009) model, applied in linear RGB
- Simulations land beside the original and never change your design; inside a Section, they stay in it
- Large designs work too
- Presets can be duplicated, renamed, and shared as JSON files; the Preset Manager includes a filter reference
- Follows your Figma theme, or pick light or dark yourself
- Keyboard friendly: tabs, menus, sliders and the preview all work with the keyboard


⚠️ Disclaimer
These simulations are approximations for design and learning. They can't show exactly how any one person sees, and they're not a medical tool, so don't use them for diagnosis or treatment.
